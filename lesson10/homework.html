Diet:

Cats are obligate carnivores, meaning they must eat meat to survive. Their diet should consist mainly of animal protein, with moderate amounts of fat and minimal carbohydrates. Key nutrients like taurine, which is essential for heart health and vision, must come from animal sources.

Hygiene:

Cats are naturally very hygienic animals, spending a significant portion of their day grooming themselves with their rough tongues. While they handle most of their hygiene needs independently, some aspects of their care, like brushing and occasional baths, still require human intervention, especially for long-haired breeds or if they get particularly dirty.

Routine:

Cats thrive on routine and predictability. Establishing consistent schedules for feeding, play, and litter box maintenance can significantly reduce stress and improve their overall well-being. Cats are crepuscular, meaning they are most active at dawn and dusk, and they typically sleep for 12-18 hours a day.

Habits:

Cats are known for their unique behaviors and habits, such as sleeping for many hours a day, grooming themselves for extended periods, and being most active at dawn and dusk. They are also known for their playful nature, fondness for boxes, and their ability to communicate through various vocalizations and body language.
